# Hi, I'm Kevin 👋

Software engineer for 14 years, based in Toronto. I build products, and through [Dorfer Tech](https://dorfer.tech) I help businesses get started with AI and turn ideas into real software.

## 🚀 What I'm building

### [LeadBounty](https://leadbounty.io)
Helps small and medium businesses grow their word of mouth by automating referral marketing.

### [LootSquad](https://lootsquad.quest) (coming soon)
A playful, pirate-themed app where you and your squad earn loot by recommending the local businesses you love. It's the consumer side of LeadBounty.

### [Katana](https://github.com/drmeph/katana)
Slice through your camera roll. A free, private, open-source iPhone app for cleaning up your photos and videos one swipe at a time. Built in SwiftUI with no dependencies, and nothing ever leaves your phone.

### [ReferralWiz](https://referralwiz.quest)
A referral ROI calculator: see what a referral program could be worth to your business.

### [BrandWiz](https://brandwiz.quest)
A tiny business simulator, as a 2D game.

### [Skilled Team Generator](https://github.com/drmeph/skilled-team-generator)
Given players ranked on several skills, builds teams that are as evenly balanced as possible (bin packing plus a k-means-style balancing pass, in Python).
